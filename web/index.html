Plain text, on your Mac, without the fuss

NeatEdit is a small, native text editor. It opens a short note or a huge log just as readily, keeps your unsaved tabs when you quit, and leaves your file exactly as it found it.

Download for Mac View the source on GitHub

Free and open source. Requires macOS 14 or later. Signed and notarized by Apple. Coming soon to the Mac App Store.

Welcome.php

Nothing to lose

Tabs you never saved are still there after you quit. Quitting never stops to ask about saving, and files reopen where you left them.

Your file stays yours

Encoding, byte order mark and line endings are written back as they were. Paste always arrives as plain text, right at the caret.

Nothing leaves your Mac

No account, no analytics, no network code at all. It's open source, so you can check that yourself.

app.log

Big logs open instantly

Files over 20 MB open in a read-only viewer that stays quick at hundreds of megabytes. Find, copy and jump to any line.

It follows a growing file like tail -f: at the bottom, new lines keep arriving; scroll up, and the view stays put.

Fix one line of a huge file

Right-click a line and choose Edit Lines. You get that line and ten either side, numbered as in the file. Save writes them straight in, and only if those lines on disk are still the ones you opened.

Colour when it helps

Basic syntax highlighting, picked from the file's name. The colours never touch your text, your undo history or a printout.
